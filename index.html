Privacy Policy

Dharussalam Primary School · Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Dharussalam Primary School ("the school", "we") collects, uses and shares information in its two mobile apps:

  • DSPS Office (com.dharussalam.office) – used by school staff to manage students, attendance, exams and school records.
  • Dharussalam (com.dharussalam.parent) – used by parents and guardians to see school news and their own children's school information.

Both apps are run by the school for its own students, parents and staff. They are not open to the general public: you can only sign in if the school has registered your phone number.

1. Information we collect

Staff (DSPS Office app)

  • Name, mobile phone number and (optionally) email address, role and classes or subjects assigned.
  • Records staff create in the app, such as attendance, marks, remarks, leave, appraisal and documentation entries.

Students (entered by staff)

  • Name, date of birth, gender, class and grade, admission details and photo.
  • Attendance, early-leave records, exam marks, remarks, achievements, appreciation ("hearts") and school fee records.
  • Guardian names, relationship and phone numbers.

Parents and guardians (Dharussalam app)

  • Mobile phone number (used to sign in).
  • Messages you send to your child's class teachers.
  • Answers you submit to online exercises and their scores.

Device information

  • A push-notification token, so we can send you notifications.
  • Basic technical information needed to keep you signed in.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the apps contain no advertising.

Device features the apps use

  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to take or attach a photo (for example a student photo, a mark sheet or an exam paper).
  • Fingerprint / face unlock (DSPS Office): optional quick unlock. This is handled entirely by your phone; we never receive your biometric data.

2. How we use information

  • To run the school: attendance, exams, reports, fees and staff administration.
  • To keep parents informed about their own children and about school news and events.
  • To let parents and teachers message each other.
  • To sign you in securely with a one-time SMS code.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share information with

Information is visible only to people who need it: staff see the records their role allows, and a parent sees only their own children. We use these service providers to run the apps:

AI features

Some staff tools send content to Google's Gemini AI service. Before sending, we limit personal information: only students' first names are sent for remarks, names are removed from report text, and phone numbers, email addresses and ID numbers are stripped out. Photos of mark sheets and exam papers are sent as images. We use Google's free service tier, under which Google may use submitted content to improve its products. Everything the AI produces is a draft that a staff member checks before it is used.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Storage and security

  • Data is stored on Supabase servers and protected by encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access rules that limit each user to what their role allows.
  • Every sign-in requires a one-time code sent by SMS.
  • Parent notifications are kept for 90 days. Other school records are kept for as long as the school needs them for its educational and legal duties.

5. Children

The apps are used by school staff and by parents and guardians, who are adults. Children do not use the apps. Information about students is entered and managed by the school and shown only to that student's own parents or guardians and to authorised staff.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • You can ask the school to see, correct or delete information about you or your child, or to remove your access to the app.
  • To delete your account or data, follow the steps in section 7 below.

7. How to delete your account and data

This applies to the Dharussalam (parent) app and the DSPS Office (staff) app, published by Dharussalam Primary School.

Steps to request deletion

  1. Send an email to dharussalamps@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", or visit or call the school office on 033 229 0022.
  2. Tell us your name, the phone number you sign in with, and which app you use. Parents, please also give your child's name and class.
  3. Say whether you want your whole account deleted or only some data (for example, your messages).
  4. We may call the registered phone number to confirm the request is really from you.
  5. We will complete the request within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and your phone number's access to the app.
  • Your notification settings, push-notification tokens and notification history.
  • Messages you have sent in the app.
  • Your answers and scores for online exercises (parents).
  • Your email address and account details (staff).

What is kept, and for how long

  • Student school records (admission details, class, attendance, marks, remarks and fees) are official school records. They are kept while the student is at the school and afterwards for as long as the school is required to keep them. Deleting a parent's app account does not delete their child's school record.
  • Guardian contact details on a student's record are kept while the child is at the school, because the school needs them to contact the family. You can ask us to update them.
  • Staff work records (such as attendance, leave and marks a teacher entered) are kept as part of the school's records.
  • Backups of the database are overwritten within 30 days, after which deleted data no longer exists anywhere.
